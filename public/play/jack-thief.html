Jack Thief

Pair up your cards, draw from your neighbour and avoid being left holding the lonely Jack. Play online with 2 to 10 friends.

How to play
  • 2 to 10 players. One random Jack is removed and the other 51 cards are dealt.
  • First, throw away every pair you hold (tap two cards of the same rank), then press Done.
  • Then take turns drawing one hidden card from the next player’s hand and throw away any new pair.
  • Whoever is left holding the unpaired Jack is the Jack Thief.

    About Jack Thief online

    Jack Thief is a pairing game where nobody wants to be left with the odd card. One random Jack is removed from the deck, so exactly one Jack has no partner. Everyone throws away their pairs, then players take turns drawing a hidden card from the next player's hand. Whoever ends up holding the unpaired Jack is the Jack Thief. Play it online in real time with two to ten friends, straight from your browser.

    The game is simple to learn but full of bluffing: you can fan out your cards, hesitate, or grin when the next player reaches for the Jack. Read the full rules and strategy guide for the details.

    Features

    • Rooms for 2 to 10 players with a four-letter code and a one-tap invite link.
    • Sort stage where you throw away your pairs, with a timer so everyone stays in step.
    • Hidden draws from the next player's hand, with a public count of thrown-away cards.
    • Fair server-side shuffle and full rule enforcement.
    • Room chat and emoji reactions that float over the table.
    • Automatic play for dropped players; reopen the page to rejoin.

    Tips for your first game

    Shuffle your hand mentally and keep a straight face: players tend to avoid the card you look nervous about. If you pick up the Jack, you cannot pair it, so hold it and hope the next player draws it.
