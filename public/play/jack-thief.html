Jack Thief

Pair up your cards, draw from your neighbour and avoid being left holding the lonely Jack. Play online with 2 to 10 friends.

How to play
  • 2 to 10 players. One random Jack is removed and the other 51 cards are dealt.
  • First, throw away every pair you hold (tap two cards of the same rank), then press Done.
  • Then take turns drawing one hidden card from the next player’s hand and throw away any new pair.
  • Whoever is left holding the unpaired Jack is the Jack Thief.
