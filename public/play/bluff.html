Bluff

Put cards face down, say what they are, and call out anyone you think is lying. Play online with 3 to 10 friends.

How to play
  • 3 to 10 players. All cards are dealt; the Ace of Spades holder opens.
  • Play 1–4 cards face down and name a rank, for example “two Aces”. The cards don’t have to match.
  • The next player can call bluff, add 1–2 more cards, or pass.
  • A called play is turned over: a lie means its player picks up the pile, the truth means the caller does.
  • If everyone else passes, the last player to add gets another go before the pile is thrown away.
  • Empty your hand to be safe. The last player holding cards loses.
