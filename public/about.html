About Getaway Card Game

A small, free home for the card games we grew up playing.

Getaway Card Game started with one question: why is it so hard to play Bhabhi Thulla with friends who are in a different city? The game needs three or more people, a deck of cards and a table, and the people you want to play with are rarely in the same room. So we built a simple online table where the only thing you need is a link.

What we believe

  • Cards should be easy. No downloads, no accounts and no tutorials you have to sit through before you can play.
  • Rules should be fair. The server shuffles with a cryptographic random number generator and enforces every rule, so nobody can play out of turn or play a card they should not.
  • Games are social. Chat, emoji reactions and automatic play for players who drop out are there so that the table stays lively.
  • Traditions matter. We write down the rules as they are really played, including the edge cases, so that the guides can be used by anyone learning the games.

The games

We are building three games: Bhabhi Thulla (playable now), Bluff and Jack Thief (guides published, online rooms in development).

Get in touch

Found a bug, disagree with a rule or want a game added? Open an issue on GitHub. The game is open source and contributions are welcome.
