Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what information Jack of All ("the site", "we") handles when you visit and play. We keep it deliberately short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Display name. The name you type is sent to the game server and shown to the other players in your room. It is held in memory while the room exists and is not linked to an account.
  • Game and chat activity. Moves, chat messages and emoji reactions are sent to the other players in your room. They are kept in memory only while the room is open and are not written to a database.
  • Technical data. Like any web server, ours may process your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and keep connections alive, and hosting providers may keep standard server logs.

What we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, we do not collect your email address, phone number or payment details, and no real money is involved in any game.

Cookies and local storage

The game uses your browser's local storage to remember your last display name and a temporary room session token, so that you do not have to retype your name and can rejoin a game after a refresh. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising and third parties

We may display advertisements served by third parties such as Google AdSense in the future. If we do, those partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can manage personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com. We will update this page before any advertising is switched on.

Children

The site is a general-audience card game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Players should avoid sharing personal details in the chat.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Questions can be sent through an issue on GitHub.
