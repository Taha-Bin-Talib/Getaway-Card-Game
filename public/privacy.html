Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Jack of All ("the site", "we", "us") collects, how it is used and the choices you have. Jack of All is a free website where friends play the card games Bhabhi Thulla, Bluff and Jack Thief together online. By using the site you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

1. Information we handle

  • Display name. The name you type before creating or joining a room. It is sent to our game server and shown to the other players in the same room. It does not need to be your real name, and we recommend that it is not.
  • Game and chat activity. Your moves, room chat messages and emoji reactions are sent to the other players in your room. They are held in the server's memory while the room is open and are not written to a database or kept afterwards.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our hosting provider and server process your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and timestamps to deliver pages and keep game connections working. Hosting providers may keep standard server logs for security and troubleshooting.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact us through GitHub or by email, we keep what you send us for as long as needed to respond.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account or password. We do not ask for your email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth or payment details. No real money, prizes or purchases are involved in any game on the site. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information.

3. How we use information

  • To run the games: create rooms, deal cards, apply the rules and show your moves to the other players.
  • To let you rejoin a game after refreshing the page or briefly losing your connection.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used so that we can improve it.
  • To show advertising that helps pay for the site (see section 5).

4. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

Our own pages use your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember your last display name, your sound preference where available, and a temporary room token so that you can rejoin a game. This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. Clearing it will not harm your device; you will simply have to retype your name.

Third parties that provide advertising or measurement on the site may set their own cookies or use similar technologies. These are described below.

5. Advertising and third parties

We use or may use Google AdSense and other advertising partners to display advertisements. These partners are third-party vendors, and they may use cookies, web beacons, device identifiers and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, to measure ad performance and to prevent fraud.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info, or in the EU at youronlinechoices.com.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Even if you opt out of personalised ads, you may still see ads; they will simply be less relevant to you.

We do not control these third parties' cookies or how they use data, and this policy does not cover their practices. Please read their own privacy policies.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Where the law requires your consent for cookies or personalised advertising, we ask for it through a consent banner before such ads or cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time through the consent settings link on the site.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with: (a) the other players in your room (display name, moves and chat); (b) service providers who host or operate the site on our behalf, such as our web hosting provider; (c) advertising partners as described above; and (d) authorities or other parties where required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the site.

7. Data retention

Game and chat data live in server memory and disappear when the room closes or the server restarts. Hosting providers' server logs are kept for the period set by the provider. Browser local storage stays on your device until you clear it.

8. International transfers

The site is hosted on servers that may be located outside your country. By using the site you understand that your information may be processed in countries whose data protection laws differ from those of your country.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not keep accounts, we generally hold very little that can be linked to you. To make a request, contact us using the details below. California residents: we do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising other than through the advertising partners described in section 5, which you can opt out of using the links above.

We honour browser signals such as Global Privacy Control where the law requires. Because there is no common standard for "Do Not Track" signals, the site does not respond to them separately.

10. Children's privacy

The site is a general-audience card game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where a higher age applies in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it. Parents and guardians should supervise young players and remind them not to share personal details in the chat.

11. Security

We use reasonable measures, such as encrypted (HTTPS) connections, to protect information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Links to other sites

The site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy can be sent through our GitHub issues page.
