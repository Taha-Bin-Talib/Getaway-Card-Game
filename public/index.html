Bhabhi Thulla

The classic South Asian card game — play online with 3 to 8 friends.

How to play
  • Everyone sits around an oval table and the 52-card deck is dealt out. Aces are high. The holder of A♠ opens the game.
  • Play goes clockwise around the table. You must follow the suit that was led.
  • If you can't follow suit, play any card — that's a Thulla. The trick stops and the player with the highest card of the led suit picks up every card on the table and leads next.
  • If everyone follows suit, the highest card wins the trick, the cards are discarded and the winner leads.
  • First-round protection: in the opening trick nobody can Thulla. If you have no spades you may throw any card lower than 10 (if you have none, you pass), and the cards are discarded.
  • Move timer: you have 1 minute per move. If you run out of time, your highest card of the suit being followed is played for you.
  • Request rule: on your turn, before you deal your card (from trick 2), you may ask the next player for their whole hand — but only if they have fewer than 5 cards. They are asked and can agree or refuse. In Elimination mode, if they agree they hand over every card and are out; in Reveal mode you just look at it. Once per turn.
  • Final duel: when only two players are left, if a player's last card is smaller than the other's they are safe and the game ends. If their last card is bigger, they must pick one of the other player's cards (blind), and play it. This repeats until one player gets rid of their cards.
  • Get rid of all your cards to be safe. The last player holding cards is the Bhabhi.
