Bhabhi Thulla

The classic South Asian card game — play online with 3 to 8 friends.

How to play
  • One 52-card deck is dealt randomly among all players. Aces are high. The holder of A♠ opens the game.
  • Play clockwise. You must follow the suit that was led.
  • If you can't follow suit, play any card — that's a Thulla. The trick stops and the player with the highest card of the led suit picks up every card on the table and leads next.
  • If everyone follows suit, the highest card wins the trick, the cards are discarded and the winner leads.
  • First-round protection: in the opening trick nobody can Thulla — players without spades simply pass and the cards are discarded.
  • Request rule: on your turn (from trick 2) you can request the next player's whole hand. In Elimination mode they hand over every card and are out; in Reveal mode you just look at it.
  • Get rid of all your cards to be safe. The last player holding cards is the Bhabhi.
