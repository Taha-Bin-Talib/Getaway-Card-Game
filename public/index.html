How it works

1. Pick a game and create a room

Press Play, choose Bhabhi Thulla, Bluff or Jack Thief, type your name and create a room. You get a four-letter code and an invite link to send to your friends.

2. Friends join

Friends open the link or type the code. Up to ten players can sit around the same table, and the host starts the game when everyone is ready.

3. Play and chat

Cards are dealt around an oval table. Use the room chat and quick emoji reactions to tease the player who just picked up the pile.

The games

Bhabhi Thulla

A shedding game for 3 to 8 players. The Ace of Spades opens, everyone must follow suit, and when you cannot, you throw a Thulla: the player holding the highest card of the suit picks up the whole table. Empty your hand to be safe; the last player with cards is the Bhabhi.

  • Follow the suit that was led
  • No suit? Throw a Thulla
  • Highest card of the led suit takes the pile
  • Two players left? Final duel

Bluff

A game of lying with a straight face for 3 to 10 players. Put cards face down and say what they are. Anyone can call you a bluffer: if you lied you pick up the pile, and if you told the truth the caller does. Empty your hand to be safe.

  • Play 1–4 face-down cards and name a rank
  • Next players add, pass or call bluff
  • All pass? You get another turn to add
  • Last player holding cards loses

Jack Thief

An Old Maid style matching game for 2 to 10 players. Throw away your pairs, then draw hidden cards from your neighbour. One Jack has no partner, and whoever is left holding it is the Jack Thief.

  • One Jack removed, 51 cards dealt
  • Throw away every pair first
  • Draw one hidden card from your neighbour
  • Left with the lone Jack? You are the Thief

Why play here?

Instant and free

Nothing to install and no account to create. Open the page, enter a name and you are at the table in seconds.

Made for groups

Real-time rooms for up to eight players, with a move timer so the game keeps moving, and automatic play for anyone who drops out so nobody is stuck waiting.

Fair shuffling

Decks are shuffled on the server with a cryptographic random number generator, and every rule is enforced by the server, so nobody can play an illegal card.

Learn as you go

Every game has a detailed written guide covering the rules, the tricky edge cases and strategy tips, so beginners can learn before they sit down.

New to card games? Start here

If you have never played Bhabhi Thulla, read the Bhabhi Thulla guide first. It takes about five minutes and explains the opening move, the Thulla, first-round protection and the final duel. If you like games of bluffing and reading people, the Bluff guide explains how to count cards and when to call. If you want something relaxed that anyone can join, the Jack Thief guide is the shortest of the three.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no accounts, no downloads and no payments.

How do I invite friends?

Create a room and copy the invite link, or tell them the four-letter room code. They enter it on the Play page.

Which games can I play online?

All three: Bhabhi Thulla (3–8 players), Bluff (3–10 players) and Jack Thief (2–10 players). Press Play and pick one.

What if someone leaves in the middle of a game?

The server plays on their behalf after a few seconds, so the game continues and they can rejoin by reopening the page.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The table is drawn to fit any screen size, in portrait or landscape.
