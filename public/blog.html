Blog & updates

News about the site, new features and rule changes, newest first.

6 November 2026

Bluff and Jack Thief are now playable online

The Play button now opens a pop-up where you choose the game, and all three games can be played online. Bluff supports 3 to 10 players and includes our extra-turn rule: when everyone else passes, the player who made the last play gets another chance to add cards before the pile is thrown away. Jack Thief supports 2 to 10 players, with a sorting phase where everybody throws away their pairs, followed by drawing hidden cards from your neighbour. Both games have the same room codes, invite links, chat and emoji reactions as Bhabhi Thulla, and the server plays on for anyone who disconnects.

30 October 2026

Room chat and quick emoji reactions

Every room now has a built-in chat, plus a row of quick emoji reactions that float over the table. You can use them in the waiting room before the game starts and during play. They are perfect for celebrating a perfectly timed Thulla or for expressing disbelief when you are handed the pile. Messages stay inside the room and are not stored after the room closes.

30 October 2026

Move timer, first-round throw and a fix for leaving after a game

Each move now has a one-minute timer that is shown on your seat. If it runs out, the server plays your highest legal card so the table is never stuck waiting for one person. We also changed the opening trick: players without a spade may throw any card lower than ten, and the whole first trick is discarded, so nobody can be Thulla'd before the game has properly started. Finally, we fixed a bug where leaving a room after a game had finished could leave the host role unassigned. Now another connected player becomes host so "Play again" always works.

27 October 2026

Oval table, bigger cards and consent-based hand requests

The table is now drawn as an oval with you at the bottom and play running clockwise. Cards are larger and easier to read, and cards played in a trick land in front of the player who played them. We also reworked hand requests: before you play a card on your turn you can ask the next player for their hand, but they now have to agree, and it is only possible when they hold fewer than five cards. The host chooses in the lobby whether an accepted request eliminates and transfers the hand, only reveals it, or is switched off.

27 October 2026

The final duel

When only two players are left, the game now switches to the final duel. A player whose last card loses the trick is safe, while a player whose last card wins must blindly pick a card from the opponent's hand and play it. The duel repeats until somebody's last card loses, which makes the ending of a game far more tense than a simple race to empty your hand.

26 October 2026

Jack of All launches

The first version of the game went live: real-time Bhabhi Thulla for 3 to 8 players, with a four-letter room code, an invite link and server-side shuffling. There is no database and no sign-up, so rooms disappear when everyone leaves.

What is coming next

Computer-controlled opponents are next on the list, so that you can practise Bluff, Jack Thief and Thulla without a full table. We will announce them here when they are ready. If you have an idea or found a problem, you can open an issue on GitHub.
